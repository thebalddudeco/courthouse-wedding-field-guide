# ActiveShot Illustration Prompt Notes

The production illustration family was generated with OpenAI image generation using the supplied visual references as style inspiration only. The original concepts were then exported into the exact ActiveShot four-color palette.

## Core direction

- Crisp editorial vector-style illustration
- Thick, slightly imperfect Ink outlines
- Playful chunky geometry and bold flat fills
- Strong silhouettes that remain readable on mobile
- Photography subjects only; no financial imagery or borrowed symbols
- No gradients, photorealism, 3D rendering, fake interface text or watermarks

## Exact palette

- Screen: `#E8E6E0`
- Ink: `#1A1A1A`
- Action: `#E47B28`
- Confirmed: `#476E3D`

## Asset concepts

- Camera body with an integrated capital A, viewfinder corners and capture dot
- Open camera bag with prepared gear
- Clipboard shot list with check marks and one must-get star
- Photographer actively framing a moving subject
- Packed gear and memory card with a confirmed check mark
