# ActiveShot Brand Guide

## Brand idea

ActiveShot is the field guide photographers open before the job and keep open while the work is happening. The identity should feel decisive, practical, energetic, and creatively credible—never corporate, precious, or generically “tech.”

**Primary tagline:** Walk in ready.

**Product line:** Photography field guides, built for the shoot.

**Three-beat message:** Prep. Shoot. Wrap.

## Logo system

The Active Frame combines four viewfinder corners, a constructed letter A, and a live orange indicator. It represents preparation becoming action inside the frame.

Use the horizontal logo for websites, decks, partnerships, and wide placements. Use the stacked logo for square or centered layouts. Use the mark alone for app icons, favicons, avatars, and small spaces.

### Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of the live indicator dot. Do not crowd it with copy, borders, or another logo.

### Minimum sizes

- Symbol: 24 px digital / 8 mm print
- Horizontal lockup: 160 px digital / 42 mm print
- Stacked lockup: 120 px digital / 32 mm print

### Never

- Stretch, rotate, outline, bevel, or add a drop shadow to the logo.
- Change the live indicator to an unrelated color.
- Put the dark logo on a low-contrast photograph.
- Typeset a replacement wordmark in another font.
- Add “.app” to the logo. Use the domain separately when needed.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Surface | Screen | `#E8E6E0` | Light backgrounds, reversed text, cards and input fields |
| Foundation | Ink | `#1A1A1A` | Primary dark surface, text and dark logo |
| Primary action | Action | `#E47B28` | Calls to action, live indicator and one loud focal point |
| Confirmation | Confirmed | `#476E3D` | Success, completion and settled states |

Action should be memorable, not constant. Ink and Screen carry most layouts; Confirmed is reserved for successful, completed or settled states. Lighter or quieter hierarchy comes from spacing, line weight, scale and transparency—not additional colors.

## Typography

**Display and wordmark:** Alfa Slab One, Regular. Use for short headlines, campaign statements, and the ActiveShot wordmark. Keep it large and give it room.

**Interface and body:** Inter, Regular through Semibold. Use sentence case for body copy and controls. Uppercase is reserved for short labels and production stages.

Avoid extra-bold body text, condensed display fonts, and long paragraphs set in Alfa Slab One.

These are the only two typefaces in the ActiveShot system. Do not introduce substitutes, decorative alternates, or platform-system fonts.

## Graphic language

- Use hard cuts, broad color fields, viewfinder corners, crop marks, sequence numbers, and thin rules.
- Favor asymmetrical editorial layouts over centered app-marketing cards.
- Keep corners mostly square; use circles only for capture, progress, or status.
- Photography should feel observed and in motion, not like generic stock imagery.
- Treat checklists, handwritten notes, contact sheets, and production marks as authentic texture.

## Voice

ActiveShot sounds like a calm photographer standing beside you—not a project-management platform.

Use direct verbs: prep, frame, move, shoot, check, wrap. Keep instructions short enough to read while working. Be confident without pretending every shoot is effortless.

**Say:** “Walk in ready.” “Your next shot.” “Mark it. Move on.”

**Avoid:** “Unlock your creative potential.” “Revolutionize your workflow.” “Seamless end-to-end solutions.”

## Social system

The supplied templates use one dominant statement, one production label, and one recognizable Active Frame. Keep headlines to one thought. Use real photography only when it adds proof or instruction; do not bury the message under decorative imagery.

Safe default cadence:

1. Field tip or posing prompt
2. Behind-the-scenes workflow
3. Feature demonstration
4. Photographer story or field note
5. Template/guide release

## Website and product

The product and marketing site share the same palette, mark, and typography. Product UI remains calmer and more functional; marketing can use larger orange fields and more aggressive display type. Primary calls to action use Action Orange with Darkroom Charcoal text.
